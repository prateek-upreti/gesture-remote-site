SyncPlay Remote — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

SyncPlay Remote is made up of a phone app and a Wear OS watch app. The watch app recognises deliberate wrist gestures (or, without a watch, the phone can recognise hand gestures with its front camera), and the phone app carries out the matching action: play/pause, next/previous, volume, flashlight, or swiping a short video. This page explains what the apps use and what they do not.

Summary

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • The developer runs no servers, and the apps include no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Nothing about you is sold or shared with the developer or anyone else.

Accessibility service (Reels control)

Reels control is optional and off until you turn it on in Android Settings, after an in-app explanation. It uses Android’s Accessibility service only to see which app is in front, and makes a single up or down swipe, or a single tap in the middle of the screen, only when you make a gesture with your watch or hand. It does not read, collect or share anything on your screen, and it does not act on its own.

Camera gestures

Camera gestures are optional and off until you tap Start, after an in-app explanation and Android’s camera permission prompt. The hand recognition (Google’s open-source MediaPipe) runs entirely on your phone. Frames are never stored, shown to anyone, or sent anywhere. A notification with Stop is shown while it is on, and Android shows its camera-in-use indicator.

What the apps never access

Location, microphone, contacts, messages, notifications, files outside the app, or your screen content. Camera images are never saved or sent.

Children

SyncPlay Remote is not directed at children under 13.
